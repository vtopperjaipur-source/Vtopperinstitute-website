About the Institute

Royd Sir & V-Topper Institute

V-Topper Institute was founded on Gopalpura Bypass, Jaipur, with one goal — make English the strongest, not the weakest, section of a competitive exam paper. Under the personal brand "Royd Sir English," the institute has built a reputation for turning English from a scoring obstacle into a scoring advantage for SSC, Bank, UPSC, CDS and NDA aspirants.

At the centre of the teaching method is Royd's Key — a proprietary system of mnemonics that helps students retain vocabulary, idioms and grammar rules far faster than rote memorisation, built specifically for how competitive exam papers actually test English.
